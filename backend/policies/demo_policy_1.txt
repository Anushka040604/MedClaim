Demo Health Insurance Policy (Sample)

1. Coverage
- Inpatient hospitalization is covered after 24 hours of admission.
- Coverage includes room rent, nursing, surgeon fees, diagnostics, medicines.

2. Exclusions
- Pre-existing diseases are excluded for the first 24 months.
- Cosmetic and elective procedures are excluded.
- OPD consultation is not covered unless specified by rider.

3. Sub-limits
- Room rent capped at 2% of sum insured per day.
- ICU rent capped at 5% of sum insured per day.

4. Pre-authorization
- Planned hospitalization requires pre-authorization approval.
